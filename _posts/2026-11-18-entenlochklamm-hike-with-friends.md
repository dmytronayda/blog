On Sunday, 15 November 2026, I went hiking to the Entenlochklamm, a gorge near Kössen in Tyrol, Austria. Anna B., Vika K., and Lana P. came along with me.

I found the route in an [Instagram Reel](https://www.instagram.com/reels/DffgKCghpf2/) and saved it as a [Komoot smart tour](https://www.komoot.com/de-de/smarttour/7278782). It's a loop trail featuring a gorge, a view of a suspension bridge, and a stop at the Schrottwiesalm (which was already closed).

# The plan

Komoot rates the route as moderate: 13.4 km, 480 m of ascent, and about 4 hours 16 minutes of walking. You need basic fitness level.

I put the plan in our group chat. We went by car, so everyone chipped in €15 for diesel. I also asked everybody to charge their phones, because we were going to take photos like an autumn photoshoot. 😁

The planned timeline:

- 09:00: meet at Fürstenried West U-Bahn station and drive off
- ~10:30: arrive at the parking lot (1.5 hours in the car)
- ~12:30: reach the top (2–2.5 hours uphill)
- ~13:15: snacks and a break at the Schrottwiesalm (30–45 minutes)
- ~15:30: back at the parking lot (about 2 hours downhill)
- 17:30–18:30: back at Fürstenried West, depending on traffic

# What Komoot recorded

I recorded the hike. Here is the [finished tour](https://www.komoot.com/de-de/tour/3395378993?share_token=a3zIVePfkUKKtBWUSt4CnyoFwHsVqXcfXQjiygsvPp6X057hb6):

- Distance: 12.9 km
- Time moving: 3 hours 13 minutes
- Ascent: 450 m
- Highest point: 890 m, lowest point: 580 m
- Average speed: 4.0 km/h

That is close to the plan: a bit shorter than the 13.4 km, and 450 m of ascent against the 480 m originally planned.

# How it went
It was an easy, enjoyable hike filled with sunshine and laughter. One annoying part was that we had to walk about 1-2 km along a car road that doesn't really have sidewalks. Otherwise, the views were simply awesome.
